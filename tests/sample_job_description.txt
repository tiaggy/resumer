Acme is hiring a Product Designer to lead the design of our AI-powered analytics platform. You will collaborate closely with engineers and product managers to shape end-to-end user flows, design clear and information-dense dashboards, and prototype AI-assisted interactions for both technical and non-technical audiences.

Responsibilities
- Own the design of new product features from discovery through launch.
- Run user research and synthesize findings into actionable design decisions.
- Build high-fidelity prototypes in Figma to validate flows before engineering.
- Define and evolve the design system used across web and embedded surfaces.
- Partner with engineering to ensure shipped experiences match the intent.

Requirements
- 4+ years of product design experience in B2B SaaS, ideally with data-heavy products.
- Strong portfolio showing end-to-end product work, not just UI polish.
- Hands-on with Figma, FigJam, prototyping tools, and modern design systems.
- Comfortable working with AI features (LLMs, agents, copilots) and grounding them in real user needs.
- Excellent written communication and ability to drive alignment across functions.

Nice to have
- Experience with developer tools, observability, or analytics products.
- Background working with founders in early-stage environments.
- Familiarity with conversion-focused design and growth experimentation.

We are a fully remote team across Europe and North America.
